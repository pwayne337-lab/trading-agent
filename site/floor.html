Playing

Why this matters

1. Wake up

GitHub Actions starts the scheduled after-close run in the cloud.

2. Safety check

Broker access, paper mode, fresh prices, working stops, and the last run are verified.

3. Manage positions

Existing holdings are handled before new risk is considered.

4. Scan

Three strategies inspect completed daily bars across 193 symbols.

5. Filter

Portfolio, market, risk, and research gates can reject any setup.

6. Size

Stop distance and a 1% risk budget determine the share count.

7. Submit

The entry, protective stop, and target are linked in a bracket order. Flagged trades wait for your approval in the hub.

8. Publish

State, equity, logs, and the dashboard are updated honestly.

The main idea

The system is designed to say “no” often. A signal is only the beginning; safety, portfolio risk, sizing, and execution rules still have to agree.
