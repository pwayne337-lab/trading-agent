How this agent decides

Everything below is read from the agent's live settings when this page is built, so it always describes what the agent is actually doing right now.

Money and risk

RuleSettingWhy
Risked per trade1% of the accountA full stop-out costs this much of the account, not this much of the position. Every position is sized backwards from it.
Largest single position25% of equityA very tight stop would otherwise size you into a position so large that one overnight gap does real damage.
Most positions at once15A ceiling on how many things can go wrong at the same time.
Total investedup to 100% of equityNever borrows. At 100% the account can be fully invested but not leveraged.
Stops trading if down20%Opens nothing new until the account recovers to within 10% of its high, and waits 60 days before reconsidering.
Refuses lookalikes0.8 correlationTwo things that move together are one bet in two names. Measured on 60 days of returns.

What it looks for

StrategyWhat it buys
pullbackBuys a dip to the 20-day EMA inside an established uptrend.
breakoutBuys a new 50-session high inside an established uptrend.
reversionBuys a two-day washout in a stock still above its 200-day average.

How it decides

RuleSettingWhy
Trend filter50-day over 200-day averageOnly buys things already trending up on the slower measure.
Stop distance0.5-3 ATRPlaced off recent volatility (14-day ATR) rather than a fixed percentage, so a calm stock gets a tight stop and a wild one gets room.
Reward sought2x the riskThe take-profit sits this many multiples of the stop distance above entry.
Maximum hold40 sessionsA trade that has gone nowhere for this long is closed and the slot given back.
Minimum liquidity$20,000,000 traded a dayBelow this the spread and the slippage eat the edge.
Assumed costs5 bps entry, 10 bps on stopsCharged in every backtest, so the tested result is after costs rather than before them.
